# Walk me through a completed study

Help me understand my FastaLake results.

- Run directory: [path]
- Study design / acquisition metadata: [path or brief description]
- Main biological question: [question]

Read AGENTS.md, docs/how-to/read-outputs.md and docs/concepts/terminology.md.
First establish what actually completed. Open the QC overview, selected-method
study matrix and peptide-evidence table. Explain the acquisition roster,
quantification method, missingness and PCA eligibility.

Trace one reported group back to its assigned peptides and local protein
members. Distinguish selected candidates, accepted peptides and reporting
groups. Do not interpret a representative as a uniquely identified species or
a blank quantity as biological absence.

Summarize the useful observations and unresolved questions. Propose additional
analysis only after checking the study design and available evidence.
